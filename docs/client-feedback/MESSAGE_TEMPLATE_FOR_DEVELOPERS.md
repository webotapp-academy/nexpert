Hi Team,

**Introducing the Daily Credibility Card System** — the next phase of Nexpert's trust platform.

### The Problem

Expert platforms typically ask experts to share static badges ("My score is 847") every day. But if the number doesn't change, there's no reason to share. The platform becomes invisible to experts' networks, and their achievements stay hidden.

### The Solution

We're building **event-triggered cards** that generate *only when something meaningful changes*:

- Crossed a credibility milestone (850 points) → Card: "🎯 You crossed 850 points"
- Moved up in rankings → Card: "📈 Moved from #42 → #31 this week"
- Certified 10 learners → Card: "💬 92% rated your sessions highly"
- Got promoted to Established band → Card: "🏅 You've earned Established status"

**Result:** 
- Experts share authentically (not promotional)
- Each card tells a real story
- Nexpert becomes the infrastructure, not the brand
- Natural, genuine engagement on LinkedIn
